## Detailed Checkpoint Profiles & Strategic Roles

### 1. Marshall Mesa Trailhead (Major Course Hub & Start/Finish)
* **Coordinates**: `39.9528393, -105.2313110`
* **Google Maps Dropped Pin (Visible Marker)**: [https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=39.9528393,-105.2313110](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=39.9528393,-105.2313110)

* **Pass Schedule**:
  * **Pass 1 (Race Start)**: `0.00 mi` (0.00 km) — Friday 08:00 PM
  * **Pass 2 (Loop 1 Finish / Loop 2 Start)**: `14.98 mi` (24.12 km) — Friday ~11:10 PM - 11:45 PM
  * **Pass 3 (Loop 2 Finish / Loop 3 Start)**: `29.97 mi` (48.23 km) — Saturday ~02:41 AM - 03:51 AM
  * **Pass 4 (Loop 3 Finish / Outbound Start)**: `44.95 mi` (72.35 km) — Saturday ~06:31 AM - 08:17 AM
  * **Pass 5 (50-Mile Course Finish)**: `49.95 mi` (80.39 km) — Saturday ~08:00 AM - 10:00 AM
* **Amenities & Services**: Full Aid Station, Crew Access, Pacer Access, Drop Bags, Restrooms, Medical Tent, Hot Food, Water & Electrolytes.
* **Description**: Located off Colorado Hwy 93 at the Marshall Mesa Trailhead parking lot. This is the operational heart of the race, serving as the staging area between every 15-mile lap and hosting all major gear transitions, night crew check-ins, and the final 50-mile finish line celebration.
